# 💰 Salary Negotiation

## Preparation

1. **Research market rates**
   - Levels.fyi
   - Glassdoor
   - LinkedIn Salary

2. **Know your value**
   - Skills inventory
   - Accomplishments
   - [[Technical Skills Roadmap]]

3. **Set target range**
   - Minimum acceptable
   - Target
   - Stretch goal

## Negotiation Tips

### Do
- Negotiate after offer received
- Express enthusiasm first
- Use silence strategically
- Get everything in writing

### Don't
- Give first number if possible
- Accept immediately
- Focus only on salary

## Script

> "Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about this role. Based on my research and experience, I was expecting something closer to $X. Is there flexibility?"

## Beyond Base Salary

- Signing bonus
- Stock/equity
- Remote work
- PTO
- Professional development budget
- Title

# 🤝 Networking Tips

## Online Networking

### LinkedIn
- Complete profile (photo, headline, about)
- Engage with posts
- Share valuable content
- Personalize connection requests

### Twitter/X
- Follow industry leaders
- Share insights
- Engage genuinely

### GitHub
- Contribute to open source
- Showcase projects
- Clean commit history

## In-Person

### Events
- Conferences
- Meetups
- Hackathons

### Conversation Tips

1. Ask about their work
2. Find common interests
3. Follow up within 48 hours

## Building Relationships

- Give before asking
- Be genuinely helpful
- Stay in touch
- Offer introductions

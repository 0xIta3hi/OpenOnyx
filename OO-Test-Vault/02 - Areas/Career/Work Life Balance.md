# ⚖️ Work Life Balance

## Boundaries

- Set work hours
- Separate workspace
- Turn off notifications
- Say no when needed

## Recovery

- Regular breaks
- Hobbies outside work
- Exercise ([[Exercise Routine]])
- Quality sleep ([[Sleep Hygiene]])

## Signs of Imbalance

- Constant fatigue
- Neglecting relationships
- No personal time
- Sunday dread

## Strategies

1. Time blocking ([[Time Blocking]])
2. Prioritize ruthlessly
3. Delegate when possible
4. Schedule personal time

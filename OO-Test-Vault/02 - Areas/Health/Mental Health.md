# 🧠 Mental Health

## Key Practices

### 1. Meditation
Regular mindfulness practice:
- 10 minutes daily
- Apps: Headspace, Calm
- See [[Meditation Practice]]

### 2. Journaling
Write daily reflections:
- Gratitude practice
- Worry dumping
- Goal tracking

### 3. Social Connection
Maintain relationships:
- Weekly calls with family
- Monthly friend meetups
- Active listening

## Warning Signs

- Persistent fatigue
- Loss of interest
- Sleep changes
- Concentration issues

## Resources

- [[Stoicism]] for resilience
- [[Sleep Hygiene]] for rest
- Professional support when needed

## Daily Checklist

- [ ] Meditated
- [ ] Journaled
- [ ] Connected with someone
- [ ] Got outside
- [ ] Adequate sleep

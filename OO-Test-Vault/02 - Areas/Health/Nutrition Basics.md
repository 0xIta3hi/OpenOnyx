# 🥗 Nutrition Basics

## Macronutrients

| Macro | Purpose | Sources |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Protein | Muscle repair | Meat, beans, eggs |
| Carbs | Energy | Grains, fruits |
| Fats | Hormones, brain | Nuts, avocado |

## General Guidelines

- Eat whole foods
- Plenty of vegetables
- Adequate protein (0.8g/kg body weight)
- Stay hydrated

## Meal Planning

1. Batch cook on Sunday
2. Prep ingredients
3. Simple recipes
4. Healthy snacks ready

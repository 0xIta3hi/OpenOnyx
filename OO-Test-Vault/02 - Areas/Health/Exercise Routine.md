# 🏋️ Exercise Routine

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Workout |
|-----|---------|
| Mon | Upper body |
| Tue | Cardio |
| Wed | Lower body |
| Thu | Rest/Yoga |
| Fri | Full body |
| Sat | Active recovery |
| Sun | Rest |

## Upper Body

- Push-ups: 3x15
- Rows: 3x12
- Shoulder press: 3x10
- Plank: 3x30s

## Lower Body

- Squats: 3x15
- Lunges: 3x12
- Deadlifts: 3x10
- Calf raises: 3x15

## Cardio Options

- Running: 30 min
- Cycling: 45 min
- HIIT: 20 min

# 🧘 Meditation Practice

## Types

### Mindfulness Meditation
Focus on breath, observe thoughts.

### Body Scan
Progressive relaxation through the body.

### Loving-Kindness
Cultivate compassion for self and others.

### Walking Meditation
Mindful walking practice.

## My Routine

**Morning**: 10 minutes mindfulness
**Evening**: 5 minutes gratitude

## Technique

1. Find quiet space
2. Set timer
3. Close eyes
4. Focus on breath
5. Notice wandering, return gently
6. No judgment

## Benefits

- Reduced stress
- Better focus ([[Deep Work Notes]])
- Emotional regulation
- Improved sleep

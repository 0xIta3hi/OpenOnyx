# 😴 Sleep Hygiene

## Why Sleep Matters

- Memory consolidation
- Physical recovery
- Emotional regulation
- Immune function

## The Science

### Sleep Stages
1. **Light Sleep** (N1, N2): 50%
2. **Deep Sleep** (N3): 20%
3. **REM Sleep**: 25%
4. **Awake**: 5%

### Circadian Rhythm
Your body's 24-hour internal clock, regulated by:
- Light exposure
- Melatonin production
- Core body temperature

## Best Practices

### Environment
- [ ] Dark room (blackout curtains)
- [ ] Cool temperature (65-68°F)
- [ ] Quiet or white noise
- [ ] Comfortable mattress

### Before Bed
- [ ] No caffeine after 2 PM
- [ ] No screens 1 hour before
- [ ] Light reading or stretching
- [ ] Consistent bedtime

### Morning
- [ ] Natural light exposure
- [ ] No snooze button
- [ ] Consistent wake time

## Tracking

Use [[Habit Tracking]] to monitor:
- Bedtime
- Wake time
- Sleep quality (1-10)
- Energy levels

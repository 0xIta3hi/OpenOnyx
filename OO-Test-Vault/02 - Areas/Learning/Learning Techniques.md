# 🎓 Learning Techniques

## Evidence-Based Methods

### 1. Active Recall

> Retrieving information strengthens memory more than re-reading.

**How to practice:**
- Close the book and write what you remember
- Use flashcards
- Practice problems without looking at solutions

### 2. Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals:
- Day 1: Learn
- Day 2: Review
- Day 4: Review
- Day 7: Review
- Day 14: Review

Tools: Anki, RemNote

### 3. Feynman Technique

1. Choose a concept
2. Explain it to a 12-year-old
3. Identify gaps in understanding
4. Simplify and use analogies

### 4. Interleaving

Mix different topics/problems instead of blocking.

```
❌ AAAA BBBB CCCC
✅ ABC ABC ABC ABC
```

### 5. Elaboration

Connect new information to existing knowledge.
Ask: "Why?" and "How does this relate to X?"

## Study Environment

- [ ] Minimize distractions
- [ ] Consistent location
- [ ] Proper lighting
- [ ] Comfortable temperature

## Time Management

### Pomodoro Technique
- 25 minutes focused work
- 5 minute break
- After 4 pomodoros: 15-30 min break

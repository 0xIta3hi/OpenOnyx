# 📈 Investment Basics

## Why Invest?

Combat inflation and build wealth over time through [[Compound Interest]].

## Asset Classes

| Asset | Risk | Return | Liquidity |
|-------|------|--------|-----------|
| Stocks | High | High | High |
| Bonds | Low-Med | Low-Med | High |
| Real Estate | Medium | Medium | Low |
| Cash | Very Low | Very Low | Very High |

## Key Concepts

### Diversification
Don't put all eggs in one basket. Spread across:
- Asset classes
- Sectors
- Geographies

### Dollar-Cost Averaging
Invest fixed amount regularly regardless of price.

### Risk Tolerance
Factors:
- Age
- Income stability
- Goals timeline
- Emotional capacity

## Investment Vehicles

### Index Funds
Track market indices. Low fees, broad exposure.
- S&P 500 funds
- Total market funds
- International funds

### ETFs
Exchange-traded funds. Like index funds but trade like stocks.

### Individual Stocks
Higher risk, requires research.

## Getting Started

1. Max employer 401(k) match
2. Build emergency fund
3. Open IRA (Roth or Traditional)
4. Start with index funds

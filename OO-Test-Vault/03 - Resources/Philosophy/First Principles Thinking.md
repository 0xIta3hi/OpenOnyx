## What Is It?

Break down problems to fundamental truths, then reason up from there.

## Process

1. **Identify assumptions**
2. **Break down to basics**
3. **Create new solutions**

## Example: SpaceX

Assumption: Rockets are expensive
First principles: What are rockets made of?
Insight: Materials are 2% of rocket cost
Solution: Build from scratch, reuse

## vs Analogy

Analogy: "It's always been done this way"
First principles: "What's actually necessary?"

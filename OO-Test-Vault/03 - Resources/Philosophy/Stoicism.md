# 🏛️ Stoicism

## Core Principles

### 1. Focus on What You Can Control
> "We cannot choose our external circumstances, but we can always choose how we respond to them." — Epictetus

**Dichotomy of Control**:
- Within control: Your thoughts, actions, values
- Outside control: Others' actions, external events

### 2. Negative Visualization
Imagine losing what you have to appreciate it more.

### 3. Voluntary Discomfort
Practice mild hardship to build resilience.

### 4. Memento Mori
Remember death to live fully.

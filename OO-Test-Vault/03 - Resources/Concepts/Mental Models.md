# 🧠 Mental Models

## Decision Making

### Second-Order Thinking
Consider consequences of consequences.

### Inversion
Think about what to avoid.

### Regret Minimization
Will I regret not doing this?

## Problem Solving

### [[First Principles Thinking]]
Break down to fundamentals.

### Occam's Razor
Simplest explanation is often correct.

### Pareto Principle
80% of results from 20% of effort.

## Learning

### Circle of Competence
Know what you know and don't know.

### Feynman Technique
Explain simply to understand deeply.

# 🤖 AI Ethics

## Key Concerns

### Bias
AI systems can perpetuate or amplify existing biases.

### Privacy
Data collection and surveillance concerns.

### Transparency
Black-box models difficult to explain.

### Job Displacement
Automation affecting employment.

### Autonomy
AI making decisions that affect humans.

## Principles

1. **Fairness**: Treat all groups equitably
2. **Transparency**: Explain how AI works
3. **Privacy**: Protect personal data
4. **Safety**: Prevent harm
5. **Accountability**: Clear responsibility

## Questions to Consider

- Who benefits? Who is harmed?
- What data is being used?
- Is there human oversight?
- Can decisions be explained?

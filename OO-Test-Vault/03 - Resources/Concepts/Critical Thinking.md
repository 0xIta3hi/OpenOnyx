# 🤔 Critical Thinking

## Core Skills

1. **Analysis**: Break down information
2. **Evaluation**: Assess credibility
3. **Inference**: Draw conclusions
4. **Explanation**: Communicate reasoning
5. **Self-regulation**: Examine own thinking

## Cognitive Biases to Avoid

### Confirmation Bias
Seeking info that confirms existing beliefs.

### Availability Heuristic
Overweighting recent/memorable examples.

### Anchoring
Over-relying on first piece of information.

### Dunning-Kruger Effect
Overestimating competence in unfamiliar areas.

## Questions to Ask

- What's the source?
- What's the evidence?
- What assumptions are being made?
- What's the counter-argument?
- Am I being objective?

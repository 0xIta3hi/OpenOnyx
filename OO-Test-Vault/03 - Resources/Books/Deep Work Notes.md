# 📖 Deep Work Notes

**Author**: Cal Newport
**Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Definition

> **Deep Work**: Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit.

vs.

> **Shallow Work**: Non-cognitively demanding, logistical tasks, often performed while distracted.

## Why Deep Work Matters

1. **Rare**: Most people can't do it
2. **Valuable**: Produces high-quality output
3. **Meaningful**: Provides satisfaction

## Strategies

### 1. Monastic Philosophy
Eliminate or radically minimize shallow work.
*Example: Donald Knuth doesn't use email*

### 2. Bimodal Philosophy
Dedicate blocks of time to deep work.
*Example: Carl Jung retreated to a tower to write*

### 3. Rhythmic Philosophy
Make deep work a daily habit.
*Example: Same time every day*

### 4. Journalistic Philosophy
Fit deep work wherever you can.
*Requires practice*

## Rules

1. **Work deeply**: Schedule blocks
2. **Embrace boredom**: Train focus
3. **Quit social media**: Be intentional
4. **Drain the shallows**: Batch and minimize

## Implementation

My deep work schedule:
- 6:00-9:00 AM: Deep work (no email)
- 9:00-10:00 AM: Shallow tasks
- 10:00-12:00 PM: Meetings
- 1:00-4:00 PM: Deep work
- 4:00-5:00 PM: Admin
